PART E — How the Meta AI works (muse-spark-1.3 + sam-3.1)

muse-spark-1.3 — what the photo is: your photo is downscaled and location-stripped on the server, sent once to POST /v1/chat/completions with a fixed instruction (seven obstacle types, evidence, limitations, confidence). The answer must match schema 1.0 or the job fails honestly — nothing invented. You get a type, a confidence bar, visible-evidence bullets, limitations and Human review required. Without a server key it fails with a clear missing-config error; the labelled Mock mode is pipeline test only.

sam-3.1 — how much of the path is blocked: the same photo gets two segmentation prompts (the paved pedestrian walkway + the obstacle phrase for its type). Boxes are clipped to the walkway and unioned into 1–100% of visible walkway in this photo — never metres, no confidence score. No box means not measured = unknown, and only BlockedSidewalk / ConstructionBarrier / Other are measured at all. Needs its own opt-in flag (two billed calls per photo); live demo has it on.

What they solve together: Muse turns an unlabelled photo into comparable evidence; SAM adds the share a classifier cannot give. Both feed evidence cards, comparisons and route explanations — ranking still uses fewest known obstacles, never the percent.
